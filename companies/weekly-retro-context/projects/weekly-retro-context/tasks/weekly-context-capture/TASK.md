Each Wednesday, gather ClickUp and GitHub activity from the previous seven
days, normalize the evidence, hand the bundle to the reviewer, and render the
final JSON list.

The output should stay focused on items that are useful for weekly retro or
postmortem conversations:

- topics that matter
- the people who participated
- the evidence that supports the inclusion
- short insights that stay grounded in the collected data
